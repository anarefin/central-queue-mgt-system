Notifications: triggers, templates, channels, quiet hours, throttle and retry

How messages are generated from a trigger catalogue, rendered from templates, sent over web push, in-app or email, suppressed, throttled and retried.

Model

Prerequisite: no notification templates are seeded. Author a template (trigger x channel x language) first, or every message is suppressed with reason no_template.

Notifications are generated asynchronously by a job worker from a trigger catalogue (FR-NTF-003: sending never blocks or delays a queue operation). Screen: Admin > Notifications (/admin/notifications/, config:service_catalogue, pick a Site).

  • Channels (Phase 1): web_push, in_app, email, and internal staff_alert. No SMS and no native push (ADR-0011). A channel with no adapter is skipped in favour of the next in the order.
  • Overrides: a trigger can be enabled and re-ordered per Site and per Service (a Service row overrides its Site row, which overrides the catalogue).
  • Templates: one per trigger x channel x language, using {{variable}} from a fixed set per trigger. Ticket triggers: token_number, service_group_name, service_name, site_name; your_turn adds counter_label; appointment triggers: token_number, service_name, site_name, date, time; staff alerts: service_group_name, counter_label, site_name. An unknown variable is rejected at save. Content never contains clinical details or account numbers (FR-NTF-034).
  • Language: the visitor's preferred language, falling back to the Site default.
  • Throttle: at most 4 messages per Ticket and 10 per visitor per day (qms.notification.max-per-ticket, max-per-day).
  • Quiet hours suppress every non-essential trigger between a Site's quiet_hours_start and _end (Site timezone); they are recorded with status quiet_hours and never sent later as a batch. Known gap: the two Site columns have no admin screen or API; set them in SQL (see the case below).
  • Opt-out: a visitor can silence non-essential triggers from their ticket page; essential ones cannot be silenced (FR-NTF-035).
  • Retry: a failed send retries with exponential backoff (backoff-base-seconds 30) up to max-attempts-per-channel (3), then falls to the next channel.
  • Delivery log: GET /notification-messages?ticket_id=&visitor_id=&status= (audit:read) lists every message and its attempts (sent / failed).

Email channel

Email is used for appointment messages, the visitor OTP and scheduled report delivery. Settings (all qms.notification.email.*): host, port (587), username, password, starttls (true), from (noreply@qms.local), connection-timeout-millis (5000). With no host the email adapter is unavailable and messages fall to the next channel.
