Service catalogue, token numbering, priority and routing

Service groups and Services, outcome codes and Teams; how token numbers are built and reset; Priority classes, defaults by channel or Service, and the routing strategy.

Service catalogue (/admin/catalogue/, config:service_catalogue)

Site > Service group > Service. A Service group is a department or clinic and owns exactly one Team of Agents. A Service is the thing a visitor queues for.

Service fieldRule
name_i18nper language, at most 200 characters, the default language is required
token_prefixat most 8, [A-Za-z0-9]+, unique among active Services at the Site
expected_minutes1-1440
sla_wait_minutes1-1440
channelsany of kiosk, reception, mobile, appointment_checkin; no duplicates
booking_modeappointment_only, walk_in_only, both
visitor_identifiernot_required, optional, mandatory
parallel servingparallel_limit 1-20, and at least 2 when parallel serving is on
Counter link preference weight1-99, default 1
Outcome codeat most 50 characters, [a-z0-9_]+

Deactivating a parent (Site or Service group) deactivates its active children. Nothing with tickets is deleted.

Token numbering (/admin/numbering/)

A Ticket number is {prefix}{separator}{sequence, zero padded}, for example B-004. One rule can be set per Service or per Service group (the Service rule wins). Digits are always Western Arabic.

SettingRuleDefault
prefix_sourceservice, service_group, priority_class, fixedservice
fixed_prefixrequired when source is fixed, at most 8, [A-Za-z0-9]+none
separatorat most 8 characters, no control characters-
padding0-6 digits3
sequence_start0-999,999,9991
reset_boundarydaily, weekly (Monday), monthly (the 1st), neverdaily
reset_timeHH:mm, Site-local00:00

Preview shows the next number without issuing one. The reset period is a pure function of the Site clock and timezone, so a sequence restarts at the reset time even if nothing was running then.

Priority and routing (/admin/priority/, config:priority_routing)

A Priority class carries a head start (minutes of virtual waiting on arrival, 0-1440), an optional maximum wait (1-1440) and an optional token prefix override. The seeded Normal class has head start 0 and is the default; it can never be deactivated. Defaults can be set by channel and by Service. The class on a Ticket is chosen by precedence: manual, appointment, visitor category, channel, Service (FR-QUE-011). The routing strategy is per Service group: weighted_wait (default), strict_priority or fifo; see Queue engine.
