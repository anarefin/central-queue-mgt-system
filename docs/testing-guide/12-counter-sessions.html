Counter sessions and the F2 to F10 serving actions

Open a session, call, re-announce, start, miss, hold, transfer, break, close and force-close, with every limit and every refusal.

The serving desk (/console/)

Role: agent (own session) or team_admin. One focused, keyboard-first screen with no sidebar. A session belongs to one Counter and one agent and is stored on the server, so a refresh, a network loss or a backend restart shows the same Ticket again (GET /sessions/current).

KeyActionButtonEnabled when
F2Call next*Call next*session open, nothing called or serving (or room for a parallel Service)
F3Re-announce*Re-announce*Ticket called, under the re-announce limit
F4Start service*Start service*Ticket called
F5Complete*Complete*Ticket serving
F6Miss*Miss*Ticket called (warns when the next Miss closes it as no_show)
F7Transfer*Transfer*Ticket serving
F8Hold*Hold*Ticket serving, under the hold limit
F9Break / End break*Break*nothing in progress (or on a break)
F10Close session*Close session*nothing called or serving and no held Tickets

Also: Call a specific Token (out of order, with a reason), Return to queue and Keep in the call-timeout card, and *Held by me* with a Resume per Ticket. Browsers may steal F5 (reload) and F10 (menu); the console cancels the default on every mapped key, ignores key repeat, and while a panel (Transfer, Break, Call specific) is open only F7 and F9 act, so typing a note cannot complete a Ticket.

Settings you will change to test limits (in your compose override, see Getting started):

SettingDefaultEffect
QMS_QUEUE_ANNOUNCE_REPEAT_LIMIT3re-announces allowed per Ticket
QMS_QUEUE_MISS_LIMIT2Misses before a Miss becomes no_show
QMS_QUEUE_MISS_REENTRY_POSITION / _AFTERafter-n / 3where a missed Ticket returns (front, after-n, back)
QMS_QUEUE_HOLD_LIMIT3 (0 = off)Tickets a session may hold
QMS_QUEUE_CALL_TIMEOUT_SECONDS90 (0 = off)unanswered call prompt
QMS_QUEUE_TRANSFER_HEADSTART_MINUTESempty = predecessor's waithead start on a successor

**Wait for the *In service* badge** after F4 before pressing F5 or F7. Both race the in-flight start request and silently do nothing if pressed too early.

Re-announce and Miss (F3, F6)

*Re-announce* replays the call and changes no state (announce_count +1, capped). *Miss* declares the visitor absent: miss_count +1, the Ticket returns to waiting and re-enters at the configured position; a Miss that would take miss_count past the limit closes it as no_show. Agents never choose no_show. Re-announce does not count towards the Miss limit.

Transfer (F7) and the successor Ticket

A transfer closes the Ticket in service as transferred (terminal) and creates a Successor ticket for another Service, Counter or agent, in one transaction. The Successor shares the Token number, the Visit, the Priority class, the channel and the ticket secret, and points back with predecessor_ticket_id. The visitor never sees a new number. Wait is attributed to each Ticket's own Service. The Successor gets a transfer head start: by default the predecessor's accrued wait, rounded to whole minutes, so the visitor is not sent to the back. A Ticket sent to a Counter or agent waits in their personal queue. Transfers are within one Site. Reports count chain heads, not rows.

An administrator with the open/close permission for everyone's sessions (System, Org, Team Admin; not an agent, not even for their own) can force-close a stale session.
