Reports, exports, schedules, retention and the BI extract

Running each report against the reporting schema, exporting to CSV, XLSX or PDF, scheduling delivery, retention and anonymisation, and reading the warehouse from a BI tool.

How reports work

Screen: Admin > Reports (/admin/reports/). Permission: reports:run_export; exporting or scheduling also needs visitor_pii:view (both are held by System, Org and Team Admin; Team Admin cannot render the page in practice, see Roles).

  • Reports never run against the live queue. They read a separate reporting schema refreshed about every 15 seconds and never more than 60 seconds behind, so a Ticket that just completed appears after the next sweep and a heavy report cannot slow a counter.
  • "Tickets issued" counts chain heads, not rows: a Ticket transferred twice is three rows in the detailed report (three waits, one per Service) but one issued Ticket (ADR-0006).
  • Percentiles come from raw Ticket rows, never averages of averages. Every report shows the period next to the previous equivalent period with absolute and percentage change.
Report keyWhat it is
detailed-tokenone row per Ticket with every timing, Counter, agent, outcome and transfers; filters by date range, Zone, group, Service, agent, class, channel, category; server-paged and sortable
visitor-flowissued, served, cancelled, no-show by hour, day, month or year, peak waiting, abandonment, channel mix
countersessions, open hours, served, idle time, utilisation
agentfull agent KPI set incl. login adherence
service, department, sitevolume, average and P90 wait, handling time, SLA attainment (each rolls up the one below)
breakbreaks per agent and type, overruns
appointment, journey, feedback, notification, auditdomain reports
peak-hours, staffing-gapplanning views

An unknown key is 404 not_found. From and To are required for period comparison.

Export

POST /reports/detailed-token/export supports csv, xlsx and pdf. A small export answers inline (ready). A large one (over qms.reporting.export.async-threshold-rows, default 50 000 rows) becomes a background job you poll (GET /reports/jobs/{id}), with an expiring download link (link-ttl-hours, default 24). Only detailed-token exports; any other key is 404. CSV cells are protected against formula injection.

Schedules

A schedule (POST /reports/schedules {report_key, cadence, format, recipients, filter}) delivers a report by email on a daily, weekly or monthly cadence (enabled toggles it). The poll runs hourly (qms.reporting.schedule.poll-cron). Each delivery is recorded (GET /reports/schedules/{id}/deliveries).

Retention and BI

Retention policies (/admin/privacy/ and GET|PUT /retention/policies/{dataClass}, audit:read) decide how long ticket detail, ticket aggregate and audit data are kept. A nightly purge (qms.retention.purge-cron, 02:30) anonymises old detail rows in the reporting schema and purges past retention, audited as retention.purge_run. The BI schema bi.ticket_fact_v1 is a stable view over the fact table with no direct visitor identifiers (docs/bi-access.md); a nightly extract ticket_fact-<yyyymmdd>.csv is written to qms.reporting.extract.location (03:00).
