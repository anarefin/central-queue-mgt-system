Appointments: templates, booking, rescheduling, waitlist, check-in and no-show

Availability templates and exceptions, transactional booking, self-service changes, the check-in window, and automatic no-shows.

Model

Appointments reserve capacity and convert into a Ticket at check-in. The whole feature sits behind the org-wide appointment flag (Banking, Healthcare and Government ship it on; Producer services and Generic off).

  • Availability is defined at three levels, most specific wins: service, team, individual agent (GET|PUT /appointment-templates/{level}/{targetId}). A slot template carries weekday (1-7), start, end, slot minutes, capacity (concurrent per slot) and a validity date range. Exceptions block a date, add extra availability, or reduce capacity (/appointment-exceptions/...).
  • Business hours and holidays suppress slots automatically. Per-Service settings: booking horizon (default 30 days), minimum lead time (default 2 hours), waitlist on or off (/services/{id}/appointment-settings).
  • Booking is transactional against remaining capacity: two simultaneous bookings for the last seat give exactly one success. A slot is held for 5 minutes while details are completed (qms.appointment.hold-minutes).
  • A visitor may book, reschedule or cancel until 2 hours before the slot (visitor-cutoff-minutes). Staff may act any time with a reason inside the cut-off.
  • A visitor may hold at most 3 active appointments (max-active-per-visitor).
  • Check-in is allowed from 30 minutes before to 15 minutes after the slot (checkin-window-before-minutes, checkin-grace-minutes). Earlier: offered a walk-in Ticket without cancelling the appointment. Later: the appointment is marked no_show.
  • An appointment Ticket gets the appointment class and a bonus of 15 minutes, and can never displace a Ticket already being served.
  • No-show policy (off by default): after 3 no-shows in 90 days online booking is blocked, walk-ins never.
  • Reminders at 24 hours and 1 hour before, in the visitor's language.
  • Every appointment gets a unique reference code and QR usable for check-in.

Sources (source): phone, walk_in, staff (staff-chosen) and visitor (set by the server for a visitor's own booking, never client-chosen).

Check-in and conversion (API only in this build)

Check-in takes the reference code (typed or from the QR). There is no check-in screen anywhere in this build: the kiosk has no appointment step and the Reception desk has no check-in button. The endpoints exist and are what an integration or a future screen calls: POST /appointments/check-in (staff, appointment:checkin) and POST /kiosk/appointments/check-in (a kiosk device). Run these cases with curl. This is recorded in Appendix E. A mobile geofence check-in belongs to remote tickets, not appointments.
