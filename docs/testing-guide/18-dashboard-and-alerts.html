Live dashboard, threshold alerts and escalation

Where is it going wrong right now: the live tiles and filters, acting from the dashboard, and the six alert thresholds with grouping, acknowledgement and escalation.

Live dashboard (/console/dashboard/)

Refreshes through the realtime channel, not polling, and shows data no more than 5 seconds stale (FR-MON-001). It needs dashboard:view_all (System and Org Admin) or dashboard:view_own_groups (Team Admin, Reception, and Agent as S); anyone else is told *You do not have permission to view the dashboard* instead of a broken page.

Filters are chosen from lists, not typed ids: Site, Zone, Service group, Service, Priority class, Agent. Service needs a group chosen first. The filter state is shareable as a URL.

TileShows
Waiting nowper Service group: *N waiting, longest 12m*
Serving now*Token at Counter - elapsed*
CountersOpen, On break, Closed, Idle with a queue waiting
Longest waitstop 10 by wait, flagged Past SLA and Escalated
Throughput todayServed, Cancelled, No-show, Transferred
Appointments todayBooked, Checked in, No-show, Upcoming in the next hour
Remote queueRemote, Approaching, Present, Forfeited
Device healthKiosks offline, Displays offline (*Printers offline: not tracked yet*)
Served per open Counterload balancing

Actions from the dashboard: *Re-prioritise* (with a mandatory reason, *Change priority*), *Force-close* a session, *Change agent status*, *Send a staff alert*.

Threshold alerts

Per Service (GET|PUT /services/{id}/alert-thresholds, config:service_catalogue) set maxima for six metrics; each left null leaves that metric unmonitored:

Threshold typeFires triggerMeaning
queue_lengthqueue_sla_breachTickets waiting exceeds the maximum
longest_waitqueue_sla_breachlongest wait exceeds the maximum
no_show_ratequeue_sla_breachno-show rate exceeds the maximum
idle_counterscounter_unattendedCounters idle with a queue waiting
device_offlinekiosk_display_offlinea kiosk or display offline longer than the maximum
break_overrunagent_break_overruna break runs past its type's maximum

group_window_minutes (default 15) groups repeat breaches into one alert; escalation_delay_minutes (default 30) escalates an unacknowledged alert to the Org Admin. The sweep runs every 30 seconds (qms.alerts.threshold-check-cron, escalation-check-cron). Alerts are listed at GET /sites/{id}/alerts and acknowledged with an optional note (POST /alerts/{id}/acknowledge).
